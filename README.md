# FIC FURY

> Live Fictional Debate & Character Roleplay

[FIC FURY](https://ficfury.com/) is a live fictional debate and character
roleplay platform where participants become characters, debate scenarios,
negotiate decisions, and influence how stories unfold.

**The story isn't already written.**

---

## What is FIC FURY?

FIC FURY brings fictional worlds into a structured, interactive debate
experience.

Instead of simply watching a story unfold, participants step into the role
of a character and make decisions that can change what happens next.

Participants can:

- Take on the role of a fictional character
- Debate scenarios within a fictional universe
- Research and prepare their position
- Present arguments and defend their side
- Negotiate with other characters
- Form alliances
- Respond to changing situations
- Draft and vote on decisions
- Influence how the story unfolds

---

## The Experience

FIC FURY combines elements of:

- **Fictional debate**
- **Character roleplay**
- **Interactive storytelling**
- **Public speaking**
- **Negotiation**
- **Persuasion**
- **Collaborative decision-making**

The experience uses a structured debate format inspired by elements of
formal debate and Model UN-style simulations, while focusing on fictional
characters and fictional scenarios.

---

## How It Works

### 1. Create an account

Join FIC FURY and enter the platform.

### 2. Explore the available experiences

Discover the fictional debate experiences currently available.

### 3. Choose your character

Take on the role of an available character and understand their perspective,
goals, relationships, and interests.

### 4. Prepare

Research the scenario, understand your character's position, and prepare
your arguments.

### 5. Enter the debate

Step into the debate and interact with the other characters.

### 6. Debate. Negotiate. Persuade.

Defend your position, form alliances, respond to opposing arguments, and
adapt as the situation develops.

### 7. Change what happens next

Your decisions and negotiations can influence the outcome of the scenario.

## Why FIC FURY?

Traditional fiction has a predetermined story.

Traditional debate has predetermined topics.

FIC FURY brings the two together.

You don't simply read the story.

You don't simply argue about it.

**You become part of it.**
