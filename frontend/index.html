02
DEBATE / REWRITTEN

THE ARGUMENT
IS THE STORY.

Traditional debate asks you to defend an argument. FIC FURY asks you to defend a character.

Instead of debating an abstract topic, you enter a fictional conflict with a character, objective and position of your own. Research the situation, speak from your character's interests, challenge opposing arguments and negotiate your next move.

MUN?

LOVE MUN OR PUBLIC SPEAKING?

FIC FURY isn't Model United Nations. But if you enjoy MUN, debate, research, diplomacy, persuasion and negotiation, you'll recognize the skills. The difference is simple: you represent a fictional character, not a country.
