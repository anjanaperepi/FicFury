FIC FURY / PRIVACY

YOUR DATA. YOUR CHOICE.

This Privacy Policy explains how FIC FURY handles information associated with your use of the platform.

LAST UPDATED: AUGUST 2026
ON THIS PAGE Information We Collect How We Use Information Information Sharing Data Security Data Retention Your Choices Policy Changes
01

INFORMATION WE COLLECT

When you create and use a FIC FURY account, the platform may collect information that you provide directly.

  • Account information such as your name, email address and role.
  • Profile information that you choose to provide.
  • Information associated with committee registrations and participation.
  • Content you submit through platform features, such as resolutions, amendments and other debate-related contributions.
02

HOW WE USE INFORMATION

Information associated with your account may be used to operate and improve FIC FURY.

This may include authenticating users, managing committee participation, displaying relevant platform content, maintaining session activity and improving the functionality of the service.

03

INFORMATION SHARING

FIC FURY does not intend to sell personal information to third parties.

Information may be processed by infrastructure and service providers that are necessary to operate the application, such as hosting, database and deployment providers.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect the security and integrity of the service.

04

DATA SECURITY

Reasonable technical measures are used to help protect account information and application data against unauthorized access, alteration or disclosure.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

05

DATA RETENTION

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the service, maintain application records, resolve disputes, enforce applicable terms or meet legal obligations.

06

YOUR CHOICES

Depending on the information involved and applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including the ability to request access, correction or deletion.

If you have a privacy-related request, please use the contact page to get in touch with the FIC FURY team.

07

POLICY CHANGES

This Privacy Policy may be updated as FIC FURY evolves. Changes will be reflected on this page along with an updated revision date.
