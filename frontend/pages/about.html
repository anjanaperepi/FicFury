FICTION MEETS DEBATE.

FIC FURY is a platform built around a simple idea: what happens when fictional worlds become the setting for serious debate?

01

WHAT IS FIC FURY?

FIC FURY is a fictional debate platform where participants step into the roles of characters from different fictional universes.

Inspired by the structure and interaction of Model United Nations, FIC FURY brings together characters, committees, debate sessions, resolutions, amendments and voting into one interactive experience.

Instead of debating as themselves, participants represent fictional characters and argue from their perspectives, interests and goals.

The result is a space where familiar characters can encounter completely unfamiliar situations and participants can explore how their decisions affect the fictional world around them.

02 / THE IDEA

GIVE EVERY CHARACTER A VOICE.

FIC FURY is designed to encourage creativity, strategic thinking, public speaking, negotiation and collaborative decision-making through fictional scenarios.

03

MORE THAN A DEBATE.

A FIC FURY session can involve much more than speaking during a debate.

COMMITTEES Enter fictional worlds built around different themes and scenarios.
CHARACTERS Represent a character and argue from their perspective.
RESOLUTIONS Turn ideas and proposals into formal resolutions.
AMENDMENTS Challenge and modify proposals during the amendment phase.
VOTING Decide which proposals ultimately shape the session.
